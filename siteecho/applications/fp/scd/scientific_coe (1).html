Official Document

Scientific Department
Code of Ethics

Version 1.0 - Base Document
Article I: Authority
All Scientific Department operations are subject to Ethics Committee oversight and approval.
Article II: Research Conduct
  • All experiments must have documented scientific justification
  • Maintain complete and accurate records
  • Follow all safety protocols
Article III: Subject Treatment
  • D-Class usage must follow established guidelines
  • Unnecessary cruelty is prohibited
  • All testing must serve legitimate scientific purposes
Article IV: Professional Standards
  • No falsification of data or scientific misconduct
  • Report conflicts of interest
  • Cooperate with other departments
Article V: Containment
  • Containment always takes priority over research
  • No experiments that compromise containment integrity
  • Follow all emergency protocols
Article VI: Violations
Violations will be investigated by the Ethics Committee and may result in disciplinary action.
BASE DOCUMENT - SUBJECT TO EXPANSION AND REVISION
This document is issued and maintained by the Ethics Committee.
All Scientific Department personnel are required to acknowledge and comply with these standards.
