Official Document

Medical Department
Code of Ethics

Version 1.0 - Base Document
Article I: Authority
All Medical Department activities fall under Ethics Committee jurisdiction and oversight.
Article II: Duty of Care
  • Provide medical attention to all Foundation personnel regardless of rank
  • Respond to medical emergencies promptly and professionally
  • Maintain sterile and safe medical environments
Article III: Patient Rights
  • All individuals receiving treatment are entitled to competent care
  • Medical treatment cannot be withheld as punishment
  • Respect patient dignity during examinations and procedures
  • Groups of Interest members found alive must receive necessary medical treatment
Article IV: Documentation
  • Keep thorough medical records for all treatments and diagnoses
  • Report unusual symptoms or anomalous medical conditions immediately
  • No alteration or destruction of medical files
Article V: Collaboration
  • Work with Scientific Department on approved medical testing
  • Coordinate with Security during containment breach injuries
  • Share critical health information with relevant departments when necessary
Article VI: Violations
Failure to comply with this Code will result in Ethics Committee review and potential disciplinary measures.
BASE DOCUMENT - SUBJECT TO EXPANSION AND REVISION
This document is issued and maintained by the Ethics Committee.
All Medical Department personnel are required to acknowledge and comply with these standards.
