Official Document

Scientific Department
Code of Ethics

Section I: Before Testing
  • All tests must have a properly formatted written test proposal.
  • All tests must be approved by the ScD Director. The Director must make sure that the tests adhere to protocols and the CoE.
  • All tests that involve a high risk of harm must undergo a review from the Ethics Committee.
  • All tests that involve Keter+ Class anomalies must undergo a review from the O5 Council.
Section II: Testing Procedures
  • All tests must be documented in detail in the proper formatting
  • ScD may only test on SCPs they have keycard access to. (Unless given special authorization by ScD Director+)
  • ScD must wear appropriate gear when testing SCPs. (e.g Hazmat, when dealing with infectious anomalies)
  • There must be at least 1 guard accompanying the scientist during tests. Higher risk SCPs means more guards! Safe-Euclid SCPs need at least 1 guard. Keter+ SCPs need at least 2 guards.
  • Guards must be briefed about specific security procedures beforehand.
Section III: Testing of SCPs
  • SCPs must be handled with exceptional care and great caution.
  • SCPs must not be damaged or harmed during the test. (unless instructed)
  • Physical or psychological harm on SCPs should be minimized.
  • Wounded/injured SCPs (assuming the SCP is sentient) must be treated according to specific procedures regarding them.
Section IV: Testing of Class D
  • Class D must be searched for contraband before leaving the Class D Cells.
  • Class D must be given clear instructions regarding the test before leaving the Class D Cells.
  • Class D must be brought back alive to Class D Cells, if possible. Unnecessary waste of life will result in an investigation!
  • Class D can only be terminated when:
    • Class D is uncooperative after multiple warnings
    • Class D is a direct and immediate threat to Foundation Personnel
    • Class D attempts escape
  • Pain and suffering of Class D should be minimized.
  • Wounded/injured Class D must be treated by medical personnel.
Section V: Quarantine
  • After an encounter with an infectious anomaly, the person is to be quarantined at the nearest quarantine facility.
  • Quarantined people are to be held separate, 1 per room, to avoid cross contamination.
  • Quarantined people are to be treated humanely, given medical attention regularly, and monitored at all times during quarantine.
Section VI: Documents
  • All research documents should be posted in research archives.
  • Secret documents can only be accessed by specifically designated researchers, O5 and EC.
  • Leaking secret documents may be a reason for termination.
GUIDELINES SUBJECT TO REVISION BY ETHICS COMMITTEE
This document is issued and maintained by the Ethics Committee.
All Scientific Department personnel are required to acknowledge and comply with these standards.
