Official Document

Security Department
Code of Ethics

Version 1.0 - Base Document
Article I: Authority
All Security Department operations are subject to Ethics Committee oversight and approval.
Article II: Primary Duties
  • Maintain secure containment of all anomalous objects and entities
  • Protect Foundation personnel and facilities from internal and external threats
  • Enforce Foundation protocols and regulations
Article III: Use of Force
  • Force may only be used when necessary to maintain security or containment
  • Excessive or unnecessary force is prohibited
  • Lethal force requires proper authorization except in immediate life-threatening situations
Article IV: Professional Conduct
  • Treat all personnel with appropriate respect regardless of classification
  • No abuse of authority or power
  • Report misconduct by fellow security personnel immediately
  • Follow chain of command
Article V: Containment Priorities
  • Containment of SCPs takes absolute priority during breaches
  • Coordinate with other departments during emergencies
  • Follow established breach protocols without deviation
Article VI: Violations
Violations will be investigated by the Ethics Committee and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
BASE DOCUMENT - SUBJECT TO EXPANSION AND REVISION
This document is issued and maintained by the Ethics Committee.
All Security Department personnel are required to acknowledge and comply with these standards.
